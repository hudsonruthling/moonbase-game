    Science note

    What is based on NASA information?

    The game uses NASA’s descriptions of lunar power challenges, radiation protection, south-pole conditions, and Moon-to-Earth communication delay. Those are facts and concepts—not live data feeds. The gameplay numbers and outcomes are simplified for play.

    Sources:

    • NASA — Living and Working on the Moon
    • NASA — Moon Base
    • NASA — Lunar Living: NASA’s Artemis Base Camp Concept (2020)
    • NASA — How NASA Will Protect Astronauts From Space Radiation at the Moon
    • Ryder & Campola — The Lunar Radiation Environment (2022)
    • NASA DONKI API and NASA Open Science Data Repository are not connected in this prototype.

    NASA describes early lunar power systems as needing to handle long periods without sunlight, and explains that regolith (loose lunar soil) can help shield a shelter. Radiation risk varies; this game does not calculate real dose. A one-way Moon communication delay of about 1.3 seconds is a light-speed calculation, not a quoted NASA measurement.
